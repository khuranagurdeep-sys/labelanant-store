Unstitched designer suits online

Label Anant brings you unstitched designer suits from Delhi, each with its dupatta and ready for your tailor. Shop embroidered suits with woven paisley dupattas, Banarasi suits woven with zari, velvet suits for winter weddings, Kalamkari crepe and floral printed suits for day functions, all with free shipping across India.

Suits for every function

Choose orange and mustard for haldi, fresh greens and teal prints for mehendi, velvet and deep evening shades for sangeet and cocktail nights, rani and red for the wedding day, and mauve, pastels and woven zari for receptions and pujas.

Why unstitched?

An unstitched suit lets your tailor shape it to your exact fit, whether you like a straight kurta, an anarkali or a short kurta with sharara. You choose the neckline, sleeves and length.
